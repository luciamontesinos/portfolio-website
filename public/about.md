## My digital self

I am a creative technologist and researcher passionate about making things you can touch, play with and get immersed in. With a technical background and creative and humanistic interests, I design and build interactive artifacts, to explore how can technology invite us to participate, create, play and pay attention to the world around us. I thrive in maker spaces where I can prototype ideas and test them in the real world. 


## My analog self

I was born and raised in Madrid, but have been living in Copenhagen for almost 6 years. My day-to-day life besides work is filled with practising futsal, dinners with friends and music. Music has been a part of my life since I first held a drumstick at age 7, and led me to teach drums to kids, play semi-professionally in several bands and record an album. When I get the chance, I love engaging in all sorts of creative projects and go on small adventures in the city or nearby nature. Another hobby I've been enjoying lately is solving jigsaw puzzles. I know, it doesn't sound as cool as being a rockstar but honestly, If there were such a thing as being a "puzzlestar", I would probably be one.
