## My digital self

I am a creative technologist, interaction designer and design researcher passionate about making things you can touch, play with and get immersed in. I just finished a PhD in Interaction Design and have a background in Software Engineering and Human-Centred AI. I design and build interactive artefacts, installations and experiences that explore how technology can invite us to participate, create, play and pay attention to the world around us. My biggest strength is my range of skills, which lets me turn an idea into a working artefact, whether it’s digital or something you can hold in your hands, and whether it means writing code, wiring electronics, designing sound and visuals, or getting hands-on with physical prototyping.


## My analog self

I was born and raised in Madrid, but have been living in Copenhagen for over 6 years. My day-to-day life besides work is filled with practising football, dinners with friends and music. Music has been a part of my life since I first held a drumstick at age 7, and led me to teach drums to kids, play semi-professionally in several bands and record an album. When I get the chance, I love engaging in all sorts of creative projects and go on small adventures in the city or nearby nature.
