## Process
Given that we had a short time to design, and needed to have a working demo, we decided to focus on a concrete scenario and design around it.  

Let's say you are hosting an event, either a hackaton or a dinner party. You want your participants or guests to know where to go when they arrive, and you want the music to match the energy of the room. Perhaps you want some calmer music when your guest are arriving and meeting each other. Or maybe you want the music to lift up the energy in the room when it's time to party. But over all, what you want the most is to spend time with your guest, without having to worry about all of these details.

What we proposed in our solution is to leverage the existing software and hardware that B&O has and create a feature that would help you have smoother events. We called it "Space Flow". In the images you can see some wireframes we quickly mocked up based on their current app. We then added a feature that allows you to create an event around the different spaces in the hosting venue and configure it around the events agenda, which you can synchronise with your calendar.

Moreover, we incorporated actions, which are sound events that trigger changes in the playback of the sound. This works using an audio feature classifier. With this, we are able to detect when someone is giving a speech, by simply recognising when someone clicks on a glass multiple times; and when the speech is over as we recognise the applause. By detecting these events, we can automaticly stop the music when the speech is about to beging and then bring the sound back once the speech is over. Simple, but effective.
